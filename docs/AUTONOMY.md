# Aisora Autonomy (candidate; publication held)

Your preferred defaults apply when a setting is absent: **Talkative**, mouse eye/head tracking on, hover reactions on, environment comments on, quiet hours on. Saved choices override defaults. Quiet hours are **22:00–08:00 on the Mac's local clock** and are configurable; equal start/end means quiet all day. This changes source/candidate builds only, never your installed app.

Open Settings → **Aisora Autonomy**. Talkative defaults to a ten-minute minimum cooldown and six interruptions per local day. Balanced uses twenty minutes and three; Quiet disables unsolicited dialogue. Mode selection resets these two controls to its defaults; you can subsequently increase the cooldown or change the daily cap. A zero daily cap silences events. Pause, Do Not Disturb and “I requested silence” persist until unchecked. Voice activity, pending/uncertain sessions (including an unknown Voice status from the paired browser), rest, inactive windows and Settings suppress new dialogue. Silence requested in spoken ChatGPT audio cannot be recognized locally; use this explicit control. No microphone transcription is added.

Events cover a sustained hover, click, return after five minutes unfocused, thirty minutes without conversation, an actual displayed-image/weather change, an optional launch greeting, an optional scheduled local hour and an optional saved follow-up reminder. Disabled triggers are discarded, never queued to interrupt later. Cooldowns and daily counts survive restart. Repeated identical triggers are suppressed. Greetings/scheduled/follow-up are off until configured; a saved topic remains local and only produces a generic reminder. Text is a small curated local fallback, **not an AI-generated reply or spoken Voice**. It disappears after eight seconds or immediately on conversation activity. Turn it off to retain only the local event pipeline.

Mouse position stays in the Aisora window. Movement events are sampled at most 15 Hz, using a projected head/hips region for approximate avatar hover (not pixel-perfect clothing collision); the animation layer smoothly blends bounded eye/head offsets. Tracking fades after two seconds without mouse movement and yields to gestures, movement, sleep and conversation. Window exit/focus loss clears the target. No global cursor hook, coordinates sent to the bridge, active-window enumeration or screen capture exists. Other applications cannot be observed.

## Integration audit and personality setup

Godot polls loopback `/v1/state` (up to 10 Hz when fresh) and sends only existing start/end/toggle commands to `/v1/command`. The Python bridge authenticates with a separate local pairing key; the selected Chrome tab is observed through visible DOM markers, with safe reconnection and duplicate-session guards. Start/stop is best-effort visible-button automation, subject to browser permissions/UI changes. Tab output RMS drives the existing mouth envelope. Unknown microphone state is not treated as proof of listening.

The extension reads neither transcripts nor ChatGPT authentication material and exposes **no supported message submission, session-context injection or proactive speech mechanism**. This feature does not add those capabilities, hidden prompts, undocumented endpoints, TTS, paid APIs, cookie access or automatic Voice starts. `proactive_dialogue_request` is a local Godot signal containing scene/time/weather/effects/action/state metadata and `speech_allowed: false`. No event is transmitted externally. Proactive Voice is explicitly shown as unavailable even when ordinary Voice is connected.

1. Create/open your Aisora ChatGPT Project in the authenticated browser.
2. In Settings, click **Copy Aisora Project instructions** and paste into the Project's instructions yourself. The complete version-controlled text is [config/aisora-personality.md](../config/aisora-personality.md). It covers her established feisty, sarcastic, intelligent, mischievous, caring personality and the illustrated garden's perception limits.
3. Open the desired conversation in that Project and pair that tab using the existing extension. Start Voice manually if its visible controls cannot be automated. Instructions are never automatically sent or read aloud; verify context in a new conversation yourself.

The local companion cannot ensure that ChatGPT retains instructions across Project/session changes. It cannot detect emotional intent or a request for silence from untranscribed Voice. Manual controls remain available. Native authenticated Voice and Ventura hardware behavior still need testing on your Mac.

## Validation and local acceptance

Automated policy and actual-VRM tests cover defaults, saved overrides, request gating, overnight/daytime quiet hours, cooldown/daily limits, repeated events, accurate displayed-state metadata, no speech flags, eye rotations, focus loss and Voice priority. Existing bridge reconnection/audio tests remain in the full suite. Rendering/performance checks are Linux Compatibility simulations, not Mac launch or authenticated Voice certification.

After a candidate is approved and installed separately, check each autonomy control survives restart, move/hover/click inside and outside the avatar, and verify no reaction in quiet hours, DND or an active Voice call. Confirm smooth eyes/head, unobscured mouth and normal lip sync during body transitions. Compare F3 frame times and Activity Monitor against your working installation at identical settings. Screen capture and active-window awareness remain unimplemented/disabled. No installed app replacement or release publication is performed by this work.
